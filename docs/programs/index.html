Programs

The IMF Programs Dataset records participation in IMF lending programs in 1952–2025. The raw dataset lists 1,693 arrangements and outright purchases for 162 IMF members, with their facilities, dates, and amounts. Most come from the IMF’s History of Lending Commitments page; the other 102 come from IMF press releases, Executive Board documents, and other IMF publications. The panel dataset turns 1,428 of these arrangements into yearly indicators for 202 independent states: months under a program, and whether one was in force, for all, concessional, and non-concessional programs. The codebook explains how the data were collected and built.

  • Panel dataset (IMFMonitor_Programs_Panel.xlsx)
    One row per independent state per year, whether an IMF member or not: the months under an IMF program, and whether a program was in force at all or for at least three, four, five, or six months, for all, concessional, and non-concessional programs. Rapid disbursements and outright purchases are left out. Further sheets document the country list, state dates, facility groups, and checks.
    Excel (.xlsx) · 912 KB · October 2026

  • Scraper script (IMFMonitor_Programs_Code_00_Webscraper.R)
    Downloads each member’s arrangements from the IMF’s History of Lending Commitments page, adds the arrangements and purchases the page does not show, and writes the raw dataset. It is provided for transparency: it takes the latest data the IMF offers, so a run today gives an updated dataset that may differ from this release, which used IMF data as of 30 September 2026.

Version history

October 2026

Replaces the hand-coded June 2020 file with a dataset built by two R scripts from the IMF’s History of Lending Commitments page, with 102 arrangements and outright purchases that the page does not show added from other IMF sources. Coverage extends from 1980–2019 to 1952–2025, and a new panel dataset gives yearly program participation indicators for every independent state. The dataset has its own codebook. The indicators have the same names as the 21 in the March 2026 Conditionality release, but are built from the new data and count months differently: a program in force from March to September of one year now counts as seven months, not six.

Current release: its files are under Downloads.

June 2020

Coded by hand from IMF documents: 1,284 arrangements in 144 countries, approved from April 1978 to December 2019. It was offered with the March 2026 Conditionality release, whose codebook lists its variables.
