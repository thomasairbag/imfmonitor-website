Conditionality

The IMF Conditionality Dataset records the policy conditions attached to IMF lending programs in 1980–2019: 65,894 conditions in 134 countries. They are taken from the Letters of Intent and Memoranda of Economic and Financial Policies that borrowing governments draft with IMF staff, found in IMF Executive Board documents and Country Reports. Each condition is classified by type, from prior actions to structural benchmarks, and into one of 13 policy areas. The main dataset gives yearly indicators for 220 countries and territories; the raw dataset lists each condition. The codebook explains how the data were collected, coded, and combined.

Version history

October 2026

Removes the 21 program participation indicators added in March 2026: program participation is now recorded in the Programs dataset. The data are otherwise unchanged, and the main and raw datasets are identical to those of July 2023. The codebook drops its sections on program participation and the programs dataset, and the code no longer needs the programs file.

Current release: its files are under Downloads.

March 2026 (v2.1)

Adds 21 indicators of IMF program participation to the main dataset: the number of months a program was in force in each year, and several yes-or-no measures of whether one was in force, each for all, concessional, and non-concessional programs. The codebook gains sections on these indicators and on the programs dataset, and a note on grouping the policy areas into broader categories. All other variables, and the raw dataset, are unchanged from July 2023.

July 2023 (v2.0)

Extends coverage from 1980–2014 to 1980–2019, and from 216 to 220 countries and territories, with 65,894 conditions in all. It also revises the coding of some conditions from 1980–2014: for example, structural performance criteria in 2009 are no longer recoded as structural benchmarks, and conditions from previously embargoed documents are added. The codebook’s section on dataset updates lists each change. This release’s files are not offered separately: its main and raw datasets are identical to those of October 2026.

September 2017 (v1.0)

First release, covering 1980–2014 for 216 countries and territories, with 58,406 conditions in the main dataset. Its codebook asks users to cite Kentikelenis, Stubbs, and King (2016).
